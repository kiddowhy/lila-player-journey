## Insight 4 — Storm eliminations are relatively uncommon

796 matches and 39 Storm Deaths compared to 2418 kills and 12885 loot

### What caught my eye

Very few eliminations are caused by the storm compared to combat.

### Evidence

- Storm death events account for only a small fraction of all recorded eliminations.
- Combat events significantly outnumber storm-related deaths.

### Actionable?

Possibly.

**Potential metrics affected**

- Match Duration
- Zone Pressure
- Rotation Timing

**Recommended actions**

- Review storm timing and damage values.
- Evaluate whether the safe zone encourages sufficient player movement.
- Adjust late-game circle behavior if stronger map pressure is desired.

### Why should a level designer care?

Storm mechanics influence player rotations and match pacing. If environmental pressure is too weak, players may not be incentivized to move, reducing strategic decision-making.
