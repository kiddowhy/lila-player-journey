## Insight 2 — Large portions of the map receive very little player traffic

### What caught my eye

Movement heatmaps reveal that several sectors remain almost unused throughout matches.

### Evidence

- The movement heatmap shows sparse activity in multiple regions.
- The analytics report identifies unused grid cells across the minimap.
- Players tend to follow a limited number of common paths.

### Actionable?

Yes.

**Potential metrics affected**

- Map Utilization
- Exploration Rate
- Match Variety
- Player Retention

**Recommended actions**

- Add loot, objectives, or environmental points of interest to low-traffic areas.
- Introduce dynamic events that encourage players to rotate through underused regions.
- Review terrain accessibility for neglected sectors.

### Why should a level designer care?

Unused areas represent valuable map space that players rarely experience. Encouraging movement into these regions increases gameplay variety and improves overall map utilization.
